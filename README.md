# AI Compliance Market & Business Case Analysis

## Overview

This project evaluates the commercial opportunity for an AI-enabled compliance automation platform targeting technology and SaaS businesses in India.

The analysis uses a consulting-style approach covering:

- Market sizing (TAM, SAM, SOM)
- Competitive benchmarking
- Financial modeling
- Scenario planning
- Assumptions and source tracking

The objective is to understand the potential market size, competitive landscape, and financial economics of a compliance automation opportunity under different business assumptions.

---

## Business Question

> What is the potential commercial opportunity for an AI-enabled compliance platform in India, and how do the market size and financial economics change under different assumptions?

---

## Methodology

### 1. Market Sizing

A top-down market-sizing framework was used to estimate:

- **TAM (Total Addressable Market):** Broad population of Indian SaaS businesses identified in the selected dataset.
- **SAM (Serviceable Available Market):** Portion assumed to be serviceable by the proposed offering.
- **SOM (Serviceable Obtainable Market):** Portion assumed to be realistically obtainable.

Base-case model:

| Metric | Base Case |
|---|---:|
| SaaS business universe | 19,852 |
| Annual revenue/customer | ₹3,00,000 |
| TAM | ₹595.6 Cr |
| Serviceable % | 40% |
| SAM | ₹238.2 Cr |
| Obtainable % | 5% |
| SOM customers | 397 |
| SOM revenue | ₹11.9 Cr |

The 19,852 figure is a directional estimate from SaaS Browser, a commercial database that discovers SaaS businesses from publicly available information. It should not be interpreted as an official government census. The database is dynamic and may change over time.

---

### 2. Competitive Benchmarking

The analysis benchmarks four compliance automation platforms:

- Sprinto
- Scrut
- Vanta
- Drata

The comparison considers:

- Target customer / positioning
- Compliance framework coverage
- Integrations
- Automation capabilities
- AI capabilities
- Pricing transparency

The analysis indicates that leading platforms are increasingly combining compliance automation with broader risk, trust, GRC and AI capabilities.

---

### 3. Financial Model

A simplified SaaS financial model was developed to estimate:

- Revenue
- Cost of Goods Sold (COGS)
- Gross Profit
- Gross Margin
- Sales & Marketing expenses
- R&D expenses
- General & Administrative expenses
- Operating Profit
- Operating Margin

Base-case assumptions:

| Metric | Base Case |
|---|---:|
| SOM customers | 397 |
| Revenue/customer | ₹3 lakh |
| COGS | 25% |
| Sales & Marketing | 25% |
| R&D | 20% |
| G&A | 10% |

Base-case output:

- Revenue: approximately **₹11.9 Cr**
- Gross Profit: approximately **₹8.9 Cr**
- Operating Profit: approximately **₹2.4 Cr**
- Operating Margin: approximately **20%**

These financial assumptions are illustrative model assumptions and do not represent the actual financial performance of Sprinto or any other company.

---

### 4. Scenario Planning

Three scenarios were modeled:

| Metric | Conservative | Base Case | Upside |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| SOM Customers | 200 | 397 | 600 |
| Revenue/customer | ₹2 lakh | ₹3 lakh | ₹4 lakh |
| COGS | 30% | 25% | 20% |
| S&M | 30% | 25% | 20% |
| R&D | 25% | 20% | 15% |
| G&A | 12% | 10% | 8% |
| Revenue | ₹4.0 Cr | ₹11.9 Cr | ₹24.0 Cr |
| Operating Profit | ₹0.1 Cr | ₹2.4 Cr | ₹8.9 Cr |
| Operating Margin | 3% | 20% | 37% |

Scenario analysis is used to test how changes in customer acquisition, pricing and cost assumptions affect the economics of the business.

---

## Key Insights

1. The identified Indian SaaS business universe provides a sizeable starting population for a compliance-focused market-sizing exercise.

2. The estimated market opportunity is highly dependent on assumptions around serviceability, customer willingness to pay and obtainable market share.

3. Competitive platforms such as Sprinto, Scrut, Vanta and Drata already offer substantial automation, integrations and AI-enabled compliance capabilities.

4. AI by itself may not provide sufficient differentiation because AI capabilities are increasingly present across competing platforms.

5. The financial model shows meaningful variation between conservative, base and upside scenarios, highlighting customer acquisition, pricing and operating-cost assumptions as important business drivers.

---

## Important Assumptions & Limitations

This is an analytical portfolio project rather than an investment recommendation or forecast.

Important assumptions include:

- The SaaS business universe is based on a third-party commercial database.
- ₹3 lakh annual revenue/customer is a model assumption rather than a published Sprinto price.
- 40% serviceability is a model assumption.
- 5% obtainable market share is a model assumption.
- COGS, S&M, R&D and G&A percentages are illustrative assumptions.
- Scenario values are designed for sensitivity testing rather than prediction.
- Competitor information is based primarily on publicly available company sources and may change as products evolve.

The Excel model is designed so that key assumptions can be changed and downstream outputs recalculate automatically.
